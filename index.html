The Digital Group and Sacco app: end-to-end features

Everything a group needs to run itself, in one app. Smart Boda serves Boda Riders and their respective SACCOs, Smart Bizna serves Business Owners and their respective Groups.

1Membership

  • Member profiles and registration
  • Officials and roles: chair, secretary, treasurer
  • Suspend, exit and member portal on the phone

2Contributions

  • Contribution types and schedules
  • Merry-go-round and welfare schedules
  • Invoices, reminders and M-Pesa payments

3Fines and penalties

  • Fine categories set by the group
  • Automatic fines for late payment
  • Fines shown on each member's statement

4Loans

  • Loan products: interest, term, limits from savings
  • Applications, guarantors and approval
  • Repayment schedule, arrears and statements

5Meetings

  • Schedule and agenda
  • Attendance register
  • Minutes and resolutions stored

6Accounting and ledgers

  • Group cash book and ledgers
  • Income and expense records
  • Cash, bank and M-Pesa account tracking

7Investments and assets

  • Group projects and assets register
  • Investment tracking and returns
  • Dividend and share-out records

8Reports

  • Member and loan statements
  • Cash flow, income statement, balance sheet
  • Contribution and fines summaries

9Alerts and communication

  • SMS and in-app reminders
  • Meeting and payment notices
  • Group announcements

All group data also feeds the County Officials Dashboard, giving the County a verified view of every funded group.

The Smart Boda Rider app: end-to-end features

Everything a boda rider or bike owner needs to run the business, in one app that works online, offline and by USSD. Smart Boda serves Boda Riders and their respective SACCOs.

1Onboarding and registration

  • Mobile number, OTP and PIN set-up
  • Rider and bike profile with plate number
  • Free trial; app and USSD access for feature phones

2Daily trade recording

  • Trip fares entered in a tap
  • Works offline and syncs automatically
  • Trips recorded by USSD or SMS

3Fuel, charging and battery swap

  • Fuel, charging and battery-swap costs
  • Swap networks such as Ampersand, Roam and Spiro
  • Odometer readings and battery range alerts

4Maintenance and asset health

  • Service log by type, such as oil changes
  • Mileage-based service reminders
  • Complete history for each bike

5Income vs expense

  • Income and expenses side by side
  • Categories: SACCO dues, loan repayment, household
  • Daily, weekly and monthly profit

6Revenue targets and streaks

  • Daily, weekly and monthly targets
  • Progress shown against each target
  • Achievement streaks that reward consistency

7Compliance and licensing

  • PSV licence and insurance certificate tracked
  • Expiry reminders before documents lapse
  • Links to NTSA TIMS, eCitizen and Huduma Centre

8Community, SACCO and Chama

  • Savings deposits to SACCO and Chama accounts
  • Dues and contributions recorded
  • Savings totals the SACCO can rely on

9History, goals and account

  • Statements for loans, SACCO standing and insurance
  • Personal goals, Send Home and data export
  • Subscription paid by M-Pesa, plus feedback

Rider records build a verified income and compliance history that SACCOs, lenders and the County can rely on.

The Smart Bizna Business Owner app: end-to-end features

Everything a business owner needs to know the real numbers, in one app that works even without internet. Smart Bizna serves Business Owners and their respective Groups.

1Onboarding and set-up

  • One-tap language choice
  • Business name and type: retail, restaurant, salon, services, wholesale
  • PIN login and a free trial day

2Sales recording

  • New sale with several items on the keypad
  • Saved instantly, fully offline
  • Sales today shown on the home screen

3Purchases and expenses

  • Supplier purchases with name, phone and amount
  • Payment method recorded
  • All running expenses tracked

4Lipa Later (credit sales)

  • Customer, amount and due date recorded
  • Ageing report for pending customers
  • Total outstanding at a glance

5Daily summary and performance

  • Sales, expenses and profit for today
  • Total sales, number of sales and average sale
  • Profit = total sales minus total expenses

6Revenue targets and goals

  • Sales targets for the business
  • Financial goals with progress tracking
  • Targets reviewed against actual results

7Savings and Send Money Home

  • Savings accounts and contributions
  • Send earnings home to family and friends
  • Remittance history on record

8Financial history and statements

  • All transactions in one view
  • Total profit across periods
  • Statements to support loan applications

9Sync, subscription and support

  • Sync status shows data is safely backed up
  • Free trial and premium subscription plans
  • Suggestions, Terms and Data Privacy Notice

Business-owner records feed the group a member belongs to, giving the County a verified view of enterprise performance.

Recommendation

Smart Bizna Groups: the record-keeping layer behind every MCEF loan

✅Groups already enrolled in the MCEF Fund

These groups should be onboarded onto the Smart Bizna Groups app, working alongside the County's Safaricom-partnered lending platform and M-PESA payments. Contributions, loans and repayments become verified group records, and arrears are flagged early to protect the fund's revolving capital.

🔎Groups currently being vetted for the Fund

These groups should be advised to enrol on the Smart Bizna Groups digital platform before approval. A verifiable history of savings and repayment discipline, together with a Readiness Score, gives the County a reliable basis for fair, evidence-based funding decisions.

The result: Makueni County lends with confidence, strengthens recovery and reduces default risk, and recycles the fund to reach more youth, women and community groups through transparent, accountable and data-driven empowerment.
